The conference is being held at the Grand Hotel Vesuvio, located on the Sorrento Peninsula. Their website is very comprehensive and if you click on Services and scroll down you can even find details of the regular shuttle service to the town centre!

There are a number of different things you can do if you have time. The hotel will be happy to assist you in booking any event and you can email them directly at concierge@vesuviosorrento.com
